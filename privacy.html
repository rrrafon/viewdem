DEM Viewer — Philippine Volcanoes & Lakes

1. Who we are

This privacy policy applies to DEM Viewer (rrrafon.github.io/viewdem/), a free web application for viewing 3D terrain of Philippine volcanoes and lakes.

2. What data we collect

We do not collect personal data. This website does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information. There is no user registration, no login, and no contact form.

3. Data processed by your browser only

All terrain meshes, textures, and metadata are loaded directly from public files hosted with the site. Any data processed (for example, camera position or selected dataset) stays entirely in your browser and is never sent to a server.

4. Cookies and tracking

This website uses Google Analytics, which places cookies on your device to measure how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects information such as your IP address (anonymized), browser type, pages visited, time spent on each page, and navigation between pages. This data helps us understand which content is most useful and improve the site.

You can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

5. Third-party content

  • Terrain data: Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (publicly available via OpenTopography).
  • Imagery: Copernicus Sentinel-2 quarterly mosaics (publicly available under Copernicus terms).
  • Buildings: OpenStreetMap building footprints (Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0).

These are public data sources and contain no personal information about you.

6. Your rights

Because we do not collect, store, or share personal data, there is no personal data for you to access, correct, or delete.

7. Children

This website is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

9. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to measure traffic and understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects information such as your IP address (anonymized), browser type, device, pages viewed, time spent on each page, and navigation between pages. This data is processed by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us through the repository: rrrafon/viewdem.
